Hedges Care is a groundbreaking intersection of technology and environmental stewardship. By combining AI-powered plant analysis with blockchain technology and scientific environmental measurement, the platform creates a comprehensive ecosystem that promotes sustainable landscaping while directly contributing to global sustainability goals.

---

## 🌟 Mission Statement
To promote biodiversity, carbon sequestration, and sustainable landscaping practices through intelligent plant health management and environmental impact measurement.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 📸 AI-Powered Plant Analysis
- **High Accuracy:** Up to 96% accuracy in species identification and health assessment.
- **Global Database:** Access to 1000+ plant species from global datasets.
- **Instant Diagnosis:** Real-time processing with a 5-stage analysis pipeline.
- **Local Context:** Multilingual support including **English**, **Swahili**, and **Yoruba**.

### 🌍 Environmental Impact & CO2 Tracking
- **Scientific Calculations:** Data-driven CO2 absorption metrics based on species, health, and size.
- **Community Dashboard:** Aggregate tracking of total carbon sequestered by the community.
- **Outbreak Heatmap:** Visual representation of regional plant disease hotspots for proactive response.
- **ROI Insights:** Financial analysis of crop losses prevented through early detection.

### 💎 NFT Integration & Marketplace
- **Environmental Provenance:** Convert verified plant analysis into unique NFTs representing real-world impact.
- **Rarity System:** Based on CO2 absorption capacity:
  - 🥇 **Legendary:** ≥ 60 kg CO2/year
  - 🥈 **Epic:** 45-59 kg CO2/year
  - 🥉 **Rare:** 35-44 kg CO2/year
  - 🎖 **Uncommon:** 25-34 kg CO2/year
  - 🎗 **Common:** < 25 kg CO2/year
- **Trading Platform:** A secure marketplace to buy, sell, and trade environmental assets on the **Polygon Network**.

### 🛒 Plant Store & Services
- **Curated Selection:** Shop for climate-appropriate plants and gardening supplies.
- **M-Pesa Integration:** Seamless mobile payments for purchases and specialist consultations.
- **Expert Access:** Book real-time consultations with certified plant care specialists.
- **Video Library:** Educational content on sustainable gardening and plant care.

## 🇰🇪 National & Global Alignment

### 15 Billion Trees Initiative (Kenya)
Hedges Care serves as a monitoring and verification tool for Kenya's goal to plant 15 billion trees by 2032. We enable:
- Individual and community contribution tracking.
- Verification of plant survival rates through AI scanning.
- Quantifying actual CO2 sequestration of new forests and hedges.

We directly contribute to the following UN Sustainable Development Goal targets:
- **15.1:** Protect and restore terrestrial ecosystems.

## 🛠 Technical Architecture

### Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React 18 (TypeScript), Vite, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI.
- **Backend:** Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Edge Functions).
- **PWA:** Optimized for offline use in remote agricultural areas.
- **AI/ML:** CNN-based vision models and environmental impact algorithms.
- **Blockchain:** Polygon for efficient, low-cost NFT transactions.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone the repo:** `git clone <repository-url>`
2. **Install dependencies:** `npm install`
3. **Environment Setup:** Configure `.env` with your Supabase credentials (see `.env.example`).
4. **Database Setup:** Run the queries in `database_schema_queries.txt` in your Supabase SQL editor.
5. **Run Development:** `npm run dev` (available at `http://localhost:8080`).

For more details, see our [Setup Guide](./Docs/developer/SETUP_GUIDE.md).
