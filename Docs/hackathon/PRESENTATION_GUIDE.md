# Hackathon Presentation Guide: Hedges Care 🎤

This guide helps your team (3-5 members) structure the 5-10 minute presentation for the Moringa School Hackathon judges, specifically focusing on the theme: **"Data for Social Good: Solving Local Community Challenges"**.

---

## 🏗 The Pitch Structure (Suggested Roles)

### 1. The Hook & The Problem (Member 1) - 1.5 Minutes
- **Opening:** "Did you know Kenya aims to plant 15 billion trees by 2032? But without data, we are planting in the dark."
- **The Local Challenge:** Focus on **Agriculture & Food Security**. Mention how small-scale farmers lose crops to late-identified diseases.
- **The Gap:** Mention the lack of **Financial Inclusion** for expert services.
- **The Purpose:** "We built Hedges Care to turn data into a survival kit for our local environment and economy."

### 2. The Solution & The Tech (Member 2) - 2 Minutes
- **The Platform:** Explain Hedges Care as an AI-powered ecosystem for "Shamba" health and CO2 tracking.
- **Data Science Innovation:** Mention the **CNN-based AI** (96% accuracy) and the **Scientific CO2 Engine**.
- **The Impact Feature:** Explain the **Harambee Impact Dashboard**—collective data for a collective goal.
- **Financial Inclusion:** Highlight the **M-Pesa Integration** for accessing professional services.

### 3. The Live Demo (Member 3) - 3.5 Minutes
- **Step 1: The Scan.** Show the AI scanning a plant. Point out the **Swahili/English toggle** for community inclusivity.
- **Step 2: The Diagnosis & ROI.** Show the treatment plan and the **ROI Calculator**. "We aren't just saving plants; we are saving livelihoods (KSh)."
- **Step 3: The Dashboard.** Show the **Disease Outbreak Heatmap** and **AI Shamba Alerts**. "This is how data protects a whole neighborhood."
- **Step 4: The NFT.** Show a minted plant NFT on Polygon. "This is a verified, permanent ledger of environmental impact."

### 4. The Business & Scale (Member 4) - 1.5 Minutes
- **Economic Empowerment:** Explain how the marketplace connects local youth/specialists to jobs via M-Pesa.
- **National Alignment:** Emphasize the alignment with the **15 Billion Trees Initiative** and **UN SDG 15**.
- **Future Vision:** Mention **Carbon Credits** and **Drone Integration** for large-scale forest data.

### 5. Conclusion & Q&A (All) - 1.5 Minutes
- **Final Note:** "In the spirit of Harambee, we are using Data for Social Good. We don't just code; we code with purpose."
- **Call to Action:** Invite the judges to scan the QR code to install the PWA.

---

## 💡 Key Talking Points for Judges

- **"Data for Social Good":** Use this phrase often. Connect every feature to a "Social Good" outcome.
- **"Financial Inclusion":** Mention M-Pesa. Judges love seeing local financial solutions integrated into tech.
- **"Harambee Spirit":** Use this cultural context to explain why the community dashboard matters.
- **"Offline-First":** Reiterate that this works for the farmer in rural Kenya with spotty internet.
