# Hackathon Presentation Guide: Hedges Care 🎤

This guide helps your team (3-5 members) structure the 5-10 minute presentation for the Moringa School Hackathon judges.

---

## 🏗 The Pitch Structure (Suggested Roles)

### 1. The Hook & The Problem (Member 1) - 1 Minute
- **Opening:** "Did you know Kenya aims to plant 15 billion trees by 2032? But how many will actually survive the first year?"
- **The Gap:** Mention the "Plant and Forget" problem. Data on survival and CO2 is currently invisible.
- **The Purpose:** "We built Hedges Care to make the invisible, visible."

### 2. The Solution & The Tech (Member 2) - 2 Minutes
- **The Platform:** Explain Hedges Care as an AI-powered monitoring platform.
- **Key Tech:** Mention React, Supabase, and the PWA (Offline) capability.
- **The "Impact" Feature:** Briefly explain how we turn a simple photo into a CO2 sequestration metric.

### 3. The Live Demo (Member 3) - 3 Minutes
- **Step 1: The Scan.** Show the AI scanning a plant. Point out the Swahili/English toggle to show inclusivity.
- **Step 2: The Diagnosis.** Show the treatment plan. "This data saves the plant."
- **Step 3: The Impact.** Navigate to the Dashboard. Show the CO2 counter and the Outbreak Heatmap.
- **Step 4: The NFT.** Show a minted plant NFT. "This is the verified record of environmental impact."

### 4. The Business & Scale (Member 4) - 1 Minute
- **National Alignment:** Emphasize the alignment with the 15 Billion Trees Initiative.
- **Monetization:** Mention the Plant Store (M-Pesa) and future carbon credits.
- **Scalability:** Explain that because it's a PWA, it works anywhere in Kenya.

### 5. Conclusion & Q&A (All) - 1 Minute
- **Final Note:** "We don't just code; we code with purpose. We don't just build apps; we build for impact."
- **Call to Action:** Invite the judges to try the demo on their own phones (via PWA).

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

- **Be "Impact-First":** Whenever you mention a feature, follow it with: "The impact of this is..."
- **Offline is a Superpower:** Mention that the app works in places where the internet is "dodgy" (very relevant for Kenya).
- **M-Pesa is King:** Highlight the M-Pesa integration as it shows a clear path to real-world economic activity.
- **Show the Heatmap:** Judges love data visualization. The Disease Outbreak Heatmap is a "purposeful" use of aggregate data.

---

## 🛠 Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Test the demo on a mobile device (PWA install).
- [ ] Ensure Supabase keys are working.
- [ ] Practice the "hand-offs" between team members.
- [ ] Prepare for the "How is the AI actually working?" question (mention the CNN-based processing pipeline).
