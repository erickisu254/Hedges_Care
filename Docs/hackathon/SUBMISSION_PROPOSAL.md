# Hackathon Submission: Hedges Care 🌿

**Theme:** Code with Purpose, Build for Impact
**Target Problem:** Environmental Degradation & Climate Change in Kenya
**Solution:** AI-Driven Sustainable Landscaping & Carbon Verification

---

## 1. The "Purpose": What problem are we solving?
Kenya has set an ambitious goal to plant **15 billion trees by 2032** to combat climate change, restore water towers, and improve biodiversity. However, planting is only half the battle. **Survival and health monitoring** are the critical missing links. 

Most tree-planting initiatives suffer from a "plant and forget" approach. Without data on survival rates, disease outbreaks, or actual CO2 sequestration, it is impossible to measure the real impact of these investments.

**Hedges Care** was built to solve this. Our purpose is to turn every citizen into an active environmental steward by providing them with the professional-grade tools needed to ensure their plants thrive and their environmental contribution is verified.

## 2. The "Impact": Turning Data into Action
Hedges Care doesn't just display data; it drives impact through:

- **From Image to Action:** A user takes a photo of a sick plant. Our AI identifies the disease and provides an immediate treatment plan. **Impact:** Prevents plant mortality and stops the spread of local agricultural pests.
- **From Metric to Motivation:** We convert raw plant metrics (height, canopy area, species) into **Verified CO2 Absorption** data. **Impact:** Quantifies individual contribution to national goals, motivating continued care through tangible results.
- **From Data to Economy (NFTs):** By minting verified plant health as NFTs, we create a "Green Ledger." **Impact:** Potential for future carbon credit monetization, turning environmental care into an economic asset for local communities.
- **From Insight to Infrastructure (Heatmaps):** Aggregated data creates a real-time **Disease Outbreak Heatmap**. **Impact:** Allows local authorities and neighbors to take preemptive action before an outbreak devastates a region's greenery.

## 3. Technical Impact & Scalability
- **PWA (Offline First):** Built to work in areas with low connectivity, ensuring that farmers and gardeners in remote areas of Kenya are not left behind.
- **Localized Context:** Support for **Swahili** and **Yoruba** ensures accessibility for diverse communities, maximizing the reach of the impact.
- **Supabase Backend:** A scalable, real-time infrastructure that can handle thousands of concurrent users and data points.

## 4. Alignment with UN SDG 15 (Life on Land)
Hedges Care is a direct implementation of SDG 15, providing a digital toolset to:
- Restore terrestrial ecosystems.
- Stop and reverse land degradation.
- Halt biodiversity loss.
